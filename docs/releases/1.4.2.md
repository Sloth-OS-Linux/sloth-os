# Sloth OS 1.4.2

*November 6, 2026*

**Download:** the 1.4.2 installer ISO is on its way; the links and the checksum follow here as soon as it is up.
[Installation guide](https://sloth-os-linux.github.io/download.html)

Already running Sloth OS? Nothing to do: this release fixes the installer. Your computer gets 1.4.2 as a normal,
small update.

## The installer

- **The install no longer stops at 80% on some laptops.** On some computers (an ASUS VivoBook, for example) the
  firmware refused to change its list of boot entries ("Cannot allocate memory"), and the install stopped at the
  very last step, every time. Now the installer makes the computer's "Sloth OS" boot entry itself and carries on if
  the firmware says no, and Sloth OS starts from the disk even without that entry.

## Known rough edges

- **Installing next to Windows can keep asking for a disk check on some computers,** even after shutting Windows
  down with Shift + Shut down. The sure way around it: in Windows, open Disk Management (right-click the Start
  button), right-click drive C: → Shrink Volume, and free at least 64 GB. The installer then puts Sloth OS into that
  free space and does not touch the Windows partition at all, so no disk check is needed.

## Requirements

A 64-bit PC that starts in UEFI mode, 4 GB of memory (16 GB for games), 64 GB of disk space, and a USB stick of
8 GB or more. Feedback: Sloth Center → Feedback.
