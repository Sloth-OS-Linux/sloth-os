# Sloth OS 2.0

*November 2026*

**Download:** (the links and the checksum come with the ISO)

Already running Sloth OS? You get 2.0 as a normal, small update, no reinstall needed. After it, a notification
tells you what is new (Sloth Center → Updates shows it too).

## TV mode

- **Steam on the whole screen, like a game console:** Steam's big-screen interface, made for a controller and a TV,
  with our own start-up video. Start it from the Start menu ("TV mode"), Sloth Center → Games, or the button next
  to the power buttons in the Mac-like and ChromeOS-like menus.
- **All your games in it:** the games that are not from Steam (Heroic's Epic, GOG and Amazon games, Flatpak games,
  Windows games) show in TV mode's library with their pictures and start from there.
- **Steam's power menu works:** Switch to Desktop brings back the desktop as you left it, with your open programs;
  Sleep, Restart and Shut Down do what they say.

## For your games

- **One library for every game** in Sloth Center → Games: Steam, Heroic, Flatpak and Windows games with their
  covers and a Play button.
- **Game mode:** while a game runs, Do Not Disturb and the best performance turn on by themselves, updates and
  backups wait, and everything goes back afterwards.
- **What's wrong with the game?** When a Steam game doesn't start, Sloth OS tells you in plain words and tries
  fixes one by one (with an Undo).

## For older and weaker computers

- **Older PCs:** Sloth OS now installs on computers that start in the old BIOS mode (it takes the whole disk there;
  installing next to Windows still needs UEFI). The same USB stick starts on old BIOS PCs and new UEFI ones.
- **A lighter desktop:** on a weak computer (little memory, few processor cores, no graphics driver) the first
  sign-in turns off animations, blur and file content search by itself, with a notification. Sloth Center →
  Appearance has a switch for it.

## Everyday help

- **"I can't open this file":** a photo, PDF, Word document or video with no app for it offers the right app
  (Gwenview, Okular, LibreOffice, VLC…), installs it with one click and opens the file. Web browsers no longer
  take photos and PDFs unless you chose them.
- **Moving over from another Linux:** the "Bring your files over" step finds another Linux on the computer too,
  and brings over your folders, browser bookmarks, the apps you used (to install here) and your Steam games with
  their saves. Nothing is changed on the other system.
- **LocalSend** in the welcome wizard: send files to your phone or another computer at home, without a cable.
- **Feedback with your privacy kept:** the attached computer information now hides your name, user name, computer
  and network names, addresses and serial numbers, and "Show what is sent" shows exactly what goes.
- **Sloth Center opens in one window:** starting it again brings the open window forward.

## Fixes and looks

- A new loading screen after sign-in: the logo is drawn in a circle, then it pulses until the desktop is ready.
- The sign-in screen's own account no longer downloads the Windows programs' runner in the background.
- Every new text is available in all 50 languages.

## Requirements

A 64-bit PC (UEFI or the older BIOS mode), 4 GB of memory (16 GB for games), 64 GB of disk space, and a USB stick
of 8 GB or more. Feedback: Sloth Center → Help and feedback → Feedback.
