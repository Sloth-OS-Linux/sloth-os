Already running Sloth OS? You get 1.6 as a normal, small update, no reinstall needed. After it, a notification
tells you what is new (Sloth Center → Updates shows it too).

## One settings app

- **Every setting in Sloth Center:** KDE's settings pages (keyboard, mouse, fonts, windows, shortcuts, the login
  screen and the rest) open inside Sloth Center now. Anything that would have opened KDE's System Settings opens
  Sloth Center instead.
- **The everyday settings first:** the sidebar starts with Wi-Fi and network, Bluetooth, Display, Sound, Power and
  battery, Appearance and Updates, then groups with a small title: Apps and games, Accounts and family, System.
- **More settings in the sidebar:** a click turns the sidebar into a list of everything else, by topic, with a Back
  row at the top. A page opens on the right like any other.
- **A search at the top:** it finds Sloth Center's pages by their name and the texts on them, in your language, and
  every KDE settings page by its name and keywords. Ctrl+F jumps there.

## New pages

- **Wi-Fi:** the networks around you, connect with a password, disconnect, forget a network, turn Wi-Fi on and off,
  and the wired connection. A wrong password is said on the page. VPN, a fixed address and company networks are a
  click away under More settings.
- **Apps:** every installed app in one list, with its size, a search, Open and Remove (and, if you like, its
  settings and saved data too). Get more apps opens the welcome wizard's app list. The old Programs page is called
  Windows programs now.
- **System status** now includes the system health check-up, and **Help and feedback** is one page, with the
  guide and the feedback form at its top. The sidebar is shorter for it.

## Fixes

- **Installer:** on some computers the install could stop at 95% with "configure failed": the system partition
  was gone for a moment while the disk was being reread. The installer now waits for it.
- **Parental controls:** "Ask for more time" now really asks a parent: a password window opens, and the child gets
  a "You got more time" notice. The website filter also covers programs that are not apps from the app store
  (anything started from the terminal, too), and the sign-in check no longer adds a technical line to the sign-in
  screen.
- **Dolphin** no longer lists the system partition under Devices: it showed the system's internal folders, and its
  Properties added up the hourly backups as if each were a full copy (hundreds of gigabytes on a small disk).
- In a virtual machine on a newer AMD processor, System status no longer warns about a service that cannot run
  there (mcelog).
- Every new text is available in all 50 languages.

## Requirements

A 64-bit PC that starts in UEFI mode, 4 GB of memory (16 GB for games), 64 GB of disk space, and a USB stick of
8 GB or more. Feedback: Sloth Center → Help and feedback → Feedback.
