# Sloth OS 1.5

*November 12, 2026*

**Download:** the 1.5 installer ISO is on its way; the links and the checksum follow here as soon as it is up.
[Installation guide](https://sloth-os-linux.github.io/download.html)

Already running Sloth OS? You get 1.5 as a normal, small update, no reinstall needed. After it, a notification
tells you what is new (Sloth Center → Updates shows it too).

## Moving over from Windows

- **Your files and bookmarks:** the welcome wizard (or Sloth Center → Disks → "Bring files over from Windows")
  copies your documents, pictures, music, videos and Saved Games, and puts your bookmarks straight into the browsers
  you installed (Firefox, Chrome, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi, Chromium and other Firefox-based ones), in a folder of
  their own.
- **Your Steam games without downloading them again:** the games already on the Windows drive are copied into the
  Steam library you choose, with their saves, and run with the Windows version.
- **The programs you used on Windows:** a list of them, each with what to install on Sloth OS instead (or that it
  runs as it is).
- **A reminder about passwords:** saved browser passwords cannot be copied safely, so the wizard says how to bring
  them over yourself.

## Sloth Center

- **BIOS and device firmware updates** (Updates page): only signed updates from the official LVFS service, and
  only when you choose to install them. A laptop must be plugged in and charged to at least 30%. If the computer has
  a BitLocker-encrypted Windows, Sloth OS asks you to have its recovery key at hand first.
- **Parental controls:** child accounts with screen time (allowed hours and a daily limit, with warnings before the
  end, even over a full-screen game), the apps a child may start, adult websites blocked with safe search turned on,
  and Windows programs allowed or not per child.
- **A guest account:** a guest signs in without a password, and everything they leave behind is gone when they sign
  out.
- **Accessibility:** text and pointer size, high contrast, less motion, the magnifier, the screen reader and sticky
  keys.
- **Power and battery:** the battery's health, the power mode, when the screen turns off and the computer sleeps,
  and what the lid and the power button do.
- **Printers and scanners:** the printers and scanners Sloth OS found by itself, a test page, the default printer,
  the print queue, and scanning.
- **Help from someone you know:** from the Help page, a friend or relative can see your screen and help over the
  internet (with RustDesk), after a plain warning about "support call" scams.
- **Game saves:** Games → Game saves backs up every game's saves every hour, with a restore per game.
- **Windows programs:** Programs → "Show the last start's log" when a Windows program does not start.

## Smaller changes

- **Create New → Website shortcut…** on the desktop and in the file manager.
- The buttons on Sloth OS's own notifications (the NVIDIA guide, the network notice) open Sloth Center again.
- Every new text is available in all 50 languages.

## Known rough edges

- **Installing next to Windows can keep asking for a disk check on some computers,** even after shutting Windows
  down with Shift + Shut down. The sure way around it: in Windows, open Disk Management (right-click the Start
  button), right-click drive C: → Shrink Volume, and free at least 64 GB. The installer then puts Sloth OS into that
  free space and does not touch the Windows partition at all, so no disk check is needed.

## Requirements

A 64-bit PC that starts in UEFI mode, 4 GB of memory (16 GB for games), 64 GB of disk space, and a USB stick of
8 GB or more. Feedback: Sloth Center → Feedback.
